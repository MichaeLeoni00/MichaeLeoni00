# Michael Leoni

**Financial markets · Applied AI · Research methods**

I study financial markets and explore how artificial intelligence can support research, organize knowledge and help investigate complex questions.

My aim is to develop practical skills through projects I can understand, test and explain. I’m here to exchange ideas, learn from others and contribute as my skills grow.

## Areas of interest

- **Financial markets and trading:** market structure, price–time relationships and the evaluation of trading hypotheses.
- **Applied AI:** research workflows, context design and the integration of tools.
- **Knowledge management:** organizing sources, preserving context and tracing how conclusions develop.
- **Data analysis and automation:** developing Python skills to investigate questions and build useful tools.

## How I approach research

I value clear questions, traceable sources and explicit assumptions. I aim to distinguish observations from interpretations, test alternative explanations and document the limits of my conclusions.

For the work I share here, I will explain the problem, the approach, the role of AI assistance and the checks performed. Exploratory findings will be identified as such.

## Exchange and collaboration

I welcome discussion with people working on financial markets, AI, data analysis and research tools.

Feedback, alternative interpretations and opportunities to learn through practical collaboration are particularly welcome.
